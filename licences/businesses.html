Why it fits a chamber or association

You're always looking for member benefits that don't cost the member a dime. Buy once, and for 12 months it's part of your new-member packet, your workshop handouts, and your member emails.

What the licence covers — and what it doesn't

Covered: printing copies for your covered member businesses • workshop and event handouts • new-member packet inserts • emailing files to your members • sharing through a private members-only channel.

Not covered: reselling the files or copies • posting files on a public website or public page • editing the pages and rebranding them as your own • sharing with non-members or other organizations (each organization needs its own licence; if you outgrow your tier, we credit what you paid toward the bigger one).

When the 12 months end: stop printing new copies — but every copy already in a member's hands is theirs to keep. Renew only if you want to; nothing renews on its own.

Por qué le conviene a una cámara o asociación

Usted siempre busca beneficios para sus miembros que no le cuesten ni un dólar al miembro. Compre una vez y, durante 12 meses, forma parte de su paquete de bienvenida, sus materiales de talleres y sus correos a miembros.

Qué cubre la licencia — y qué no

Cubre: imprimir copias para sus negocios miembros cubiertos • materiales para talleres y eventos • inclusiones en el paquete de bienvenida de miembros nuevos • enviar los archivos por correo a sus miembros • compartirlos por un canal privado solo para miembros.
