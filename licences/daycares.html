A licence for your center and the families you serve

Christopher DeWitt's Shop sells printable downloads for families. A licence lets your center buy one bundle and share it with your enrolled families — printed or as files — for a full 12 months. Everything comes in English and Spanish, both included in the same licence.

Why it fits a daycare or preschool

You hand paper to parents every day — and most of it ends up in the car seat. This bundle is the kind that survives: a family emergency binder that lives on the fridge, chore charts that back up the routines you teach, a first-year log parents treasure. It's a small thing that makes your center look like it thinks about families beyond the school day — in both of their languages.

  • Family Emergency Info Binder — emergency contacts, medical info, and pickup details in one place at home
  • Kids' Chore Chart & Reward Tracker Pack — home routines that match your classroom ones
  • Weekly Meal Planner & Grocery Budget Sheet — a real help for busy parents

Every item includes its full Spanish edition at no extra cost. Bought one family at a time, this bundle costs $26.

What the licence covers — and what it doesn't

Covered: printing as many copies as you need for your covered families • welcome packets, take-home folders, and parent resource tables • emailing files to your enrolled families • sharing through a private parent app or portal.

Not covered: reselling the files or copies • posting files on a public website or public page • editing the pages and rebranding them as your own • sharing with other centers or locations (each location needs its own licence; if you outgrow your tier, we credit what you paid toward the bigger one).

When the 12 months end: stop printing new copies — but every copy already in a family's hands is theirs to keep. Renew only if you want to; nothing renews on its own.
