Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

The short version: this shop counts visits anonymously, takes your email only if you give it to us, and lets Beacons and Stripe handle checkout — we never see or store your card number, and we never sell personal data.

Who runs this shop

This website (dewittinfl1-create.github.io) is the storefront of Christopher DeWitt's Shop, run by Christopher DeWitt. It sells digital downloads (printable planners, trackers, spreadsheets and guides) and done-for-you services. Questions about this policy: email dewitt3089@hotmail.com.

What we collect — and why

  • Visit counts. We use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly counter, to see how many people visit each page and roughly where they came from. It works in aggregate — it does not build a profile of you and does not track you across other websites.
  • Your email address — only if you give it. If you join the email list (for example, to get the free Weekly Money Snapshot sample), we receive the email address you enter in the Google Form. If you email us, we see your address so we can reply.
  • Order information. Checkout, payment and file delivery are handled by Beacons and Stripe. They share the order details we need to deliver your purchase and help you afterwards — your name, email address, and what you bought. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Your language choice. If you switch this site between English and Spanish, that choice is stored in your own browser (local storage) so the site remembers it. It stays on your device.

What we do NOT collect

  • There are no user accounts on this site, so we hold no passwords or profiles for you here.
  • We do not run advertising trackers, and we do not buy or sell personal data. We never sell your information — to anyone, ever.
  • We do not ask for sensitive information (health, financial account details, government IDs) through this site.

Cookies

This site does not set advertising cookies. GoatCounter counts visits without using cookies. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard technical server logs (see GitHub's privacy statement). When you move to checkout (Beacons), or follow a link to Amazon or Walmart, those sites use their own cookies under their own policies.

Affiliate links (FTC disclosure)

Some links on this site are affiliate links to Amazon and Walmart. If you buy through one of those links, we may earn a commission — it never changes the price you pay. We point this out here and near the picks themselves, because we'd want to know.

Who your information is shared with

Only the services that make the shop run, and only what each one needs: Beacons (storefront, checkout and download delivery), Stripe (payment processing), Google Forms (email-list signups), GoatCounter (anonymous visit counts) and GitHub (website hosting). We do not share your information with marketers, and we do not sell it.

How long we keep it

If you join the email list, we keep your email address until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove it. Order records are kept as long as needed for customer support and ordinary business and tax records. Visit counts are kept only as combined totals.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe anytime — every email-list message includes an unsubscribe link, and leaving takes one click.
  • Ask about your data — email dewitt3089@hotmail.com to ask what we hold about you, or to have your email address deleted from the list. We'll take care of it.
  • Block cookies — your browser settings can block cookies on any site, including the third-party sites linked from here; this site itself will keep working.

Children

This shop is meant for adults buying planners, guides and services. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
